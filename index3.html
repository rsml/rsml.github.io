Why this works

The page remains a normal document. The deck is simply an alternate viewing mode that makes scanning easier, while preserving readability.

How it avoids gimmick

No kinetic typography. No parallax layers. One mapping only: scroll progress to slide index. Motion is optional, reversible, and never required to access the text.
